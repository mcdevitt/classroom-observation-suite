Many COPUS teams rely on a familiar Excel workbook for session summaries. This tool keeps live entry fast and avoids formula/copy-paste drift mid-class, while exporting both a COPUS-style interval matrix CSV (easy to open or paste into Excel) and a collapsed summary CSV.

Reminder: Multiple codes can be active in the same 2-minute window, so collapsed percentages reflect proportion of intervals where each group of behaviors appeared—not mutually exclusive minutes.
